The experience opportunity

The future experience

An agentic journey with visible human control.

1Express intentDescribe the outcome in plain language or start from a proven pattern.
2Resolve contextInfer partner, geography, format, localization, and eligibility.
3Build configurationRecommend safe defaults, explain why, allow correction.
4Simulate before publishPreview output, downstream impact, and exceptions.
5Govern and learnKeep approval explicit; improve reusable patterns.

Failure → AI investigation → suggested fix → human approval → execution.
